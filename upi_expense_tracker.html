How Your App Works — Beginner's Step-by-Step Guide

1. The UPI SMS Regex Parser (parseUpiSms())

When a bank sends a UPI message, it follows predictable patterns like Rs. 450.00 debited or credited with INR 2500. Our JavaScript function uses Regular Expressions (Regex) to scan the text for:

  • Amount: Matches (?:Rs\.?|INR|₹)\s*([\d,]+(?:\.\d{1,2})?)
  • Type (Debit vs. Credit): Checks for words like debited / spent / paid / sent vs. credited / received / deposited.
  • Merchant / VPA & Ref ID: Extracts the UPI ID (like swiggy@ybl) and the 12-digit UPI Reference number.

2. Connecting to Your Phone's Message App

For privacy, mobile browsers don't allow websites to silently read all your private SMS messages in the background. You have two easy ways to use this on your phone:

  • Copy & Paste (Instant): With Auto-Log on Paste checked, pasting any bank SMS immediately records it.
  • Automatic SMS Forwarding (Next Step!): Our app also listens for ?sms=... in its web link! Using a free Android automation app (like MacroDroid or Tasker) or iOS Shortcuts, whenever an SMS containing "UPI" arrives, your phone can automatically open this app with the message text and record it hands-free.

3. Day-wise, Calendar Month & Yearly Grouping

Every transaction is stored with an ISO date (YYYY-MM-DD). The app filters your list by matching the full date (2026-10-16) for Day view, the year+month (2026-10) for Calendar Month view, or the 4-digit year (2026) for Yearly view.

4. Local Storage & Backup Files

All records are saved in your browser's localStorage so they stay intact when you close or refresh the page, and the Backup tool converts that list into downloadable .json and .csv files.

Important for Android 14, 15 & 16: "Allow Restricted Settings"

Starting in Android 14, Android blocks sideloaded APKs from reading SMS or notifications until you unlock Restricted Settings once:

  1. After installing the APK, open Settings → Apps → UPI Pulse.
  2. Tap the ⋮ (Three Dots) menu in the top-right corner and tap Allow restricted settings (verify with your fingerprint/PIN).
  3. Grant SMS Permission and enable UPI Pulse Notification Listener so the app captures every UPI SMS & GPay/PhonePe/Bank alert in real time—even when the app is closed!
